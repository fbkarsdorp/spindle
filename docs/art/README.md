# Spindle illustrations

Folk papercut silhouettes. Shapes are `fill="currentColor"`; the few `class="accent"` details
expect CSS such as `.accent { fill: var(--red) }`. Cut-outs are masks with namespaced ids
(`art-<name>-…`), so all files can be inlined on one page together.

- `A.svg` – Mythological Motifs: radiant sun with a face
- `B.svg` – Animals: two birds facing each other on a branch, tulip between them
- `C.svg` – Tabu: arched, closed door with keyhole and strap hinges (the forbidden chamber)
- `D.svg` – Magic: jewelled ring with sparkles
- `E.svg` – The Dead: lantern of the dead with a candle flame
- `F.svg` – Marvels: castle on a cloud
- `G.svg` – Ogres: horned troll head with tusks
- `H.svg` – Tests: crouching sphinx on a plinth
- `J.svg` – The Wise and the Foolish: owl on a branch
- `K.svg` – Deceptions: sitting fox with curled tail
- `L.svg` – Reversal of Fortune: spoked wheel of fortune
- `M.svg` – Ordaining the Future: braided thread of fate between two spindles, shears below
- `N.svg` – Chance and Fate: pair of dice with a four-leaf clover
- `P.svg` – Society: crown
- `Q.svg` – Rewards and Punishments: pair of scales
- `R.svg` – Captives and Fugitives: tower with a braid hanging from its window
- `S.svg` – Unnatural Cruelty: rose on a thorny bramble
- `T.svg` – Sex: two interlaced hearts with a sprig
- `U.svg` – The Nature of Life: hourglass
- `V.svg` – Religion: chapel with steeple between cypresses
- `W.svg` – Traits of Character: hand-mirror with a face
- `X.svg` – Humor: jester's cap with bells
- `Z.svg` – Miscellaneous Groups: interlaced three-ring knot (Borromean) rosette
- `hero.svg` – 640×360 landing centrepiece: tree of tales with birds, two foxes, sun, moon and stars
- `spindle.svg` – app logo: drop spindle with wound cop, whorl and a loop of thread
- `owl-empty.svg` – 200×160 sleeping owl on a branch under the moon (no results)
- `divider.svg` – 600×40 symmetric vine border with a central rosette
- `corner.svg` – 80×80 corner flourish (vines along two edges, tulip pointing inward)
